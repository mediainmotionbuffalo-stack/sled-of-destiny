# Sled of Destiny

A realistic first-person 3D sledding game that runs right in your browser.

**Play it:** https://mediainmotionbuffalo-stack.github.io/sled-of-destiny/

Ride an endless mountain that winds at random through pine forest, alpine bowls, frozen rivers and the odd village (Santa's Village in the Christmas theme). Pick a difficulty from Kids to Alpine Pro. Chain tricks, dodge deer, moose, avalanches and the yeti, earn coins, upgrade your sleds and buy specialty rides like the Rocket Toboggan and Santa's Sleigh.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| ← → or A D | Lean and steer |
| ↓ or S | Tuck for speed (tap when stuck to push off) |
| Space or ↑ | Hop |
| Shift | Brake. In the air, hold with the arrows for spins and flips |
| C | Look back |
| V | Rider view or chase view |
| O | Goggles on or off (see a little farther in heavy snow) |
| P or Esc | Pause |

Gamepads work too, and phones and tablets get on-screen touch buttons.

## Challenge a friend

After a run, type your name and press **🏁 Challenge a friend** to copy a link. Your friend gets the exact same hill, conditions and difficulty, races a ghost of your run, and can send a challenge back.

## VR (Meta Quest)

Open the play link in the Meta Quest Browser. The game sees the headset and shows one big **Ride in VR** button; you pick your rider, conditions and sled inside the headset, standing by the chalet fire (left stick to choose, A for next, B to go back). On a computer there's no VR button at all.

Three view modes (pick on the Sled step, or press X while paused): **Full feel** moves and shakes like the computer camera; **Medium** (the default for new riders) keeps the horizon level through tricks with softer motion; **High** is the steadiest, for anyone new to VR.

| Control | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Left thumbstick | Steer |
| Right thumbstick (in the air) | Spins (left/right) and flips (forward/back) |
| Either trigger, or X | Trick button: hold with the right stick on the ground to spin a saucer or tube |
| Right grip | Tuck for speed |
| Left grip | Brake |
| A | Jump (and ride again after a crash-out) |
| B | Goggles (when paused: leave VR) |
| Y (hold) | Look back |
| Click left thumbstick | Pause and resume |
| While paused | X: view mode (Full feel, Medium, High) · A: graphics level · click right stick: lean-to-steer |
| After a run | A: ride again · X: change sled & conditions · B: leave VR |

Your coins, sleds, upgrades and best-run ghost are saved in your own browser.
